Features

Everything you need
to show your work.

A clean foundation you can fork, customize, and turn into your own developer portfolio.

01

Public Profiles

Give every developer a clean public portfolio accessible through a username-based route.

02

Project Showcase

Highlight your best projects with descriptions, roles, tags, images, and links.

03

Skills

Showcase the technologies, tools, and skills you use across your projects.

04

Dashboard

A starter dashboard is included as the foundation for future portfolio editing.

05

Responsive

Designed to look clean across desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

06

Open Source

Fork the project, modify the code, build new features, and make Devfolio your own.

Architecture

Simple now.
Powerful later.

Devfolio starts as a lightweight portfolio starter and is structured to grow into a complete platform.

/

Demo Portfolio

A ready-to-use portfolio experience that demonstrates the core Devfolio design and components.

/dashboard

Portfolio Dashboard

A starter editor designed to become the control center for creating and managing developer portfolios.

/u/[username]

Public Profiles

Username-based portfolio pages that can later be connected directly to a database.

lib/demo-data.ts

Easy Customization

Change the demo profile, skills, projects, links, and other portfolio information from one place.
